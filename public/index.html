Questions

What does RivalWatch monitor?

Product and price changes, plus new pages and promotions, on the competitor sites you add. Pages are fetched as static HTML.

How often does it crawl?

On a schedule you can also trigger on demand with “Crawl now”. Each page fetched uses one crawl credit.

Can I try it without a real competitor?

Yes. Every account includes three editable test competitors, so you can change a price and see it detected.

Is the AI digest required?

No. With an Anthropic key configured, Claude writes the digest; otherwise a rule-based digest produces the same 3–5 actions.
