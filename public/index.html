    How it works

    From their website to your next move

    Three steps, running on autopilot.

    • Crawl

      A scheduled crawler fetches each competitor's key pages (rendering JavaScript when it has to) and stores a snapshot.

    • Detect

      Snapshots are diffed to find price changes, new or removed products, promotions and pages. Every change is kept forever.

    • Act

      An AI step writes a short digest with 3–5 concrete, prioritised actions.

    FAQ

    Questions

    What does RivalWatch monitor?

    Product and price changes (including sales and stock), plus new pages, categories and promotions, on the competitor sites you add.

    How often does it crawl?

    On a schedule you choose for each competitor, and on demand with “Crawl now”. Each page fetched uses one crawl credit.

    Can I try it without a real competitor?

    Yes. The live demo includes three editable test competitors, so you can change a price and see it detected.

    Is the AI digest required?

    No. With an AI key configured an analyst writes the digest; otherwise a rule-based digest produces the same 3–5 actions.
